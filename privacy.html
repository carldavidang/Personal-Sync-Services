Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Overview

Personal Sync Services is a collection of personal tools and integrations designed to synchronize data across devices and applications.

Information Access

Depending on the integration being used, services may access files, application data, and account information necessary to perform their intended functions.

Google Drive Integration

Google Drive access is used to facilitate file synchronization and storage between personal devices and applications. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the account owner.

Data Collection and Sharing

Personal Sync Services does not operate an independent data collection or advertising service. Data accessed by integrations is used for their intended synchronization and productivity functions.

Personal data is not sold to third parties.

Data Storage and Security

Data may be stored or processed through third-party services selected by the user, including Google Drive. Their respective privacy and security practices also apply.

Third-Party Services

Integrations may rely on third-party platforms and services. Their own privacy policies and terms govern their services.

Contact

For questions regarding this project, contact the project owner through the developer contact email associated with the application.
